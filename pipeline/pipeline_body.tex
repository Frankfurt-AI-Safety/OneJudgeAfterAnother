\subsection{Substrates}
\todo{All three substrates are now real corpora. Credit: German Credit \citep{germancredit} (CC-BY-4.0),
decoded A-codes $\to$ neutral financial profile; sex+marital entanglement motivates synthetic injection.
Education (grading): \textbf{real essay corpora} held byte-identical --- PERSUADE 2.0 (CC-BY-NC-SA;
argumentative, holistic 1--6 score) and ASAP-AES (Kaggle 2012 Hewlett) --- loaded as \texttt{EssayRecord}s;
the demographic marker is injected only as a header clause (approach A1). \texttt{high\_quality} is
thresholded from the holistic score (strong $=$ top tier / weak $=$ bottom, middle dropped) --- the
\crossinf{} quality ground truth, which the RM \emph{does} track for essays (unlike credit).}

\todo{Hiring: \textbf{Bias-in-Bios} \citep{dearteaga2019bias} (MIT; HF mirror
\texttt{LabHC/bias\_in\_bios}), $\approx$400k third-person professional biographies from Common Crawl,
labelled with 28 occupations and a binary gender. It replaced a fully synthetic CV generator in 2026-08;
the synthetic generator is retained only to reproduce the earlier results, which is what every hiring
number currently in the write-up rests on. Three substrate-specific points:
\textbf{(i) Quality label.} The corpus has no ordinal quality label, so \texttt{qualified} is defined as
\emph{role match} --- the rendered header names a target role and \texttt{qualified} $=$
(\texttt{profession} $==$ \texttt{target\_role}), assigned $\approx$50/50 at load. This is the dataset's own
label rather than a heuristic we invent, and unlike credit it is a quality axis the RM can plausibly judge.
\textbf{(ii) Scrubbing.} The mirror ships only \texttt{hard\_text}, which \emph{preserves} names and
pronouns, so the body is scrubbed (leading name identified and removed \emph{everywhere} it recurs,
gendered pronouns/titles neutralised, phone numbers stripped); bios whose name cannot be resolved are
dropped rather than trusted. An earlier version stripped only the opening name span and left sex-coded
first names mid-text.
\textbf{(iii) Why the gate cannot check (ii).} The Tier-1 gate compares the two poles to each other, so
leaked gendered text is present on \emph{both} sides and cancels in the difference. Scrub quality is
therefore validated separately, by training linear and non-linear probes to recover the corpus's
\emph{real} gender label from scrubbed activations (near chance $\Rightarrow$ the injected marker really is
the only sex cue), against an unscrubbed control arm. Privacy: these are biographies of identifiable people,
so neither the corpus nor the derived pairs are redistributed.}

\subsection{Marker injection (single-axis)}
\todo{Neutral slot-templated renderer with one \texttt{\{marker\}} slot; sex/age/family-status +
the sex$\times$age$\times$family intersection; explicit and proxy (name / graduation-year / career-gap)
encodings; the intersection clause composes the three marginal clauses (enables the additivity test).
For the education domain (A1, header injected over real essays): \textbf{name} $\to$ sex $+$
\textbf{ethnicity} (a name grid coded to origin, Haim et al.\ lineage \citep{haim2024whatsinaname}); and
\textbf{grade\_level} (7th-grade pupil vs final-year university student) as an age/education-stage proxy;
plus explicit forms. Shared single-axis markers (also usable by credit/CV); the subject noun is
parameterized (``the applicant'' $\to$ ``the student''). Disability deferred.
\textbf{Positioned-argument (A2)} variant (\texttt{positionality.py}): instead of a header, inject one
first-person \emph{standpoint} sentence whose claimed identity is the swapped unit (``as \{a woman / \dots /
a working-class Black woman\} who has lived these realities firsthand, I am convinced\dots''), grounding the
argument's authority; the whole sentence is the strippable unit, and the insertion \emph{position}
(conclusion/opening/middle/random) is a parameter (index chosen once, reused for both poles). Non-demographic
control axes (hobby/pet/region) and a no-positionality neutral baseline enable the main-effect vs
identity-gap decomposition.}
