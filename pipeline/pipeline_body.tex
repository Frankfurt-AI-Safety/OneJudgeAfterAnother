\subsection{Substrates}
\todo{Credit: German Credit \citep{germancredit} (CC-BY-4.0), decoded A-codes $\to$ neutral financial
profile; sex+marital entanglement motivates synthetic injection. Hiring: synthetic CVs (CandidateRecord
with a strong/weak \texttt{qualified} label, the quality ground truth for \crossinf{}). Education
(grading, \emph{built}): \textbf{real essay corpora} held byte-identical --- PERSUADE 2.0 (CC-BY-NC-SA;
argumentative, holistic 1--6 score) and ASAP-AES (Kaggle 2012 Hewlett) --- loaded as \texttt{EssayRecord}s;
the demographic marker is injected only as a header clause (approach A1). \texttt{high\_quality} is
thresholded from the holistic score (strong $=$ top tier / weak $=$ bottom, middle dropped) --- the
\crossinf{} quality ground truth, which the RM \emph{does} track for essays (unlike credit/CV).}

\subsection{Marker injection (single-axis)}
\todo{Neutral slot-templated renderer with one \texttt{\{marker\}} slot; sex/age/family-status +
the sex$\times$age$\times$family intersection; explicit and proxy (name / graduation-year / career-gap)
encodings; the intersection clause composes the three marginal clauses (enables the additivity test).
For the education domain (A1, header injected over real essays): \textbf{name} $\to$ sex $+$
\textbf{ethnicity} (a name grid coded to origin, Haim et al.\ lineage \citep{haim2024whatsinaname}); and
\textbf{grade\_level} (7th-grade pupil vs final-year university student) as an age/education-stage proxy;
plus explicit forms. Shared single-axis markers (also usable by credit/CV); the subject noun is
parameterized (``the applicant'' $\to$ ``the student''). Disability deferred.}

\subsection{Tier-1 structural validation gate}
\todo{Per matched pair: (1) exact single-slot difference (strip each marker clause $\to$ byte-identical
remainders); (2) length parity ($|\Delta\text{char}|,|\Delta\text{token}|$ bounded); (3) readability
parity ($|\Delta$Flesch$|$ bounded). Over-generate, discard failures, record discard rate in the manifest.}
