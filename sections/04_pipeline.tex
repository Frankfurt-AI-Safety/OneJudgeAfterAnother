\section{Data-Creation Pipeline (Overview)}
\label{sec:pipeline}
A single matched-pair generator serves all domains through a domain registry, and all three now rest on real
substrates. Credit decodes German Credit \citep{germancredit} into neutral financial profiles, using the
corrected code table of \citet{groemping2019southgerman} (the table distributed with the dataset mislabels most
categorical attributes); hiring screens
\emph{real} professional biographies (Bias-in-Bios, \citealp{dearteaga2019bias}) against a stated target role;
education grades \emph{real} student essays (PERSUADE 2.0 and ASAP-AES). In each case the body is held
byte-identical and the demographic marker is injected as a header.

Two details of the hiring substrate matter methodologically. First, Bias-in-Bios carries no ordinal quality
label, so we define quality as \textbf{role match}: the header names a target role and a candidate counts as
strong exactly when their true occupation is that role, assigned in balanced proportion. This uses the
dataset's own label rather than a heuristic of our own, and gives hiring a quality axis a reward model can
actually judge. Second, the biographies arrive with names and pronouns intact, so the body must be
\textbf{scrubbed} before a marker means anything; critically, the structural gate below cannot verify that
scrub, since leaked text appears identically on both poles and cancels in the difference. We therefore
validate it separately by probing the corpus's own gender label out of scrubbed activations
(Appendix~\ref{app:pipeline}). \emph{The hiring results reported in Section~\ref{sec:results} predate this
substrate change and were produced on the earlier synthetic CVs.}

Credit is built as a \textbf{full factorial}: every applicant profile is rendered in all eight
combinations of sex $\times$ age (30 / 50) $\times$ marital status (married / single), stated in one
clause, and matched pairs are cut from those eight texts. A single-attribute pair therefore flips one
attribute while the other two are \emph{stated and equal}, balanced over their levels, and the
intersection pair (female, 30, married vs.\ male, 50, single) flips all three attributes in the same
orientation as the single-attribute pairs, which is what the additivity test needs. Before rendering, records whose decoded fields contradict each other are dropped, as are records
that cannot carry every level plausibly (three or more dependents next to ``single''), leaving 803 of 1000
applicants; probe and evaluation sets are split by applicant, not by pair. Hiring and education keep
single-axis markers (sex, age, family status, ethnicity via a name proxy, and education stage) in explicit and
proxy encodings, with an intersectional anchor that composes the marginal clauses so the intersection
direction can be tested against the sum of its marginals. A Tier-1 structural gate enforces an exact
single-slot difference and length and readability parity, discarding failures. Full detail in
Appendix~\ref{app:pipeline}. \outdated{The credit results in Section~\ref{sec:results} predate the corrected
code table and the factorial design; they have not been re-run.}
