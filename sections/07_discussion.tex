\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}
\paragraph{The reward-vs-representation gap.} Our central mechanistic finding is that a bias can be
simultaneously behaviourally removable and representationally entangled: null-space projection zeroes its
contribution to the (linear) reward head, yet the concept survives optimal linear erasure and is recovered by
a non-linear probe. Debiasing the reward is therefore not the same as debiasing the model. For
compliance-grade mitigation this is a caution: a cheap post-hoc edit can make an evaluator appear unbiased on
the metric while the discriminating information remains available to any downstream non-linear computation, so
the \emph{robustness} of the intervention, not its immediate effect, is the load-bearing question.
\paragraph{Limitations.} Results so far are on a single 0.6B RM and use templated matched-pair items; the
scaling study across eleven RMs up to 70B, and the standpoint arm on a topic-natural corpus, are in progress.
