\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Protected-attribute bias in language reward models is, on our preliminary evidence, behaviourally removable
from the reward scalar yet representationally entangled: nulling fixes the reward, not the representation.
Alongside this we obtain an interpretable reliability-harm metric on essay grading and a novel pro-marginalized
standpoint-credibility effect. Whether these findings, and the robustness of the mechanistic intervention,
hold on the frontier-scale reward models actually deployed is the open question our ongoing scaling study
addresses.
